## 🚀 Key Trends in AI (February 2026)

### 1. **Agentic AI Revolution**
- Autonomous AI agents are now mainstream for business workflows
- Multi-agent systems coordinate complex tasks without human intervention
- Agent-to-agent communication protocols are standardized

### 2. **AI Security is Critical**
- Every AI startup needs robust security from day one
- Prompt injection attacks are the #1 vulnerability
- Compliance frameworks (EU AI Act, US regulations) are mandatory

### 3. **Multimodal Everything**
- All major AI models now handle text, images, audio, and video
- Real-time processing is the new standard
- Cross-modal reasoning (e.g., analyzing video + audio + text) is common

### 4. **Open Source Catching Up**
- Open models like Llama 4 and Mistral Large rival closed models
- Self-hosting AI is economically viable for many use cases
- Fine-tuning on custom data is accessible to small teams

### 5. **AI-Native Startups**
- Traditional SaaS is being rebuilt with AI-first architecture
- Valuation models now heavily weight AI moats and data advantages
- Investors prioritize teams with AI/ML expertise
